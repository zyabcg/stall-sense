# Stall Sense

Tells a market vendor how much of each item to bring next market day.

Snap a photo of your handwritten sales tally. A local Gemma vision model reads it into a table, and a local TabPFN model forecasts what to load next time. Everything runs on your own laptop, offline, at no cost per run.

Status: in development for the Hacktoberfest 2026 Open-Source AI Challenge (Week 2: Side Hustle). Data in `data/synthetic/` is simulated.
